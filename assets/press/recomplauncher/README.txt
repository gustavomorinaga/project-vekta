RecompLauncher badge

badge-dark.svg and badge-dark.png are RecompLauncher's own "Get it on RecompLauncher" badge, from its press kit
(https://recomplauncher.com/press), copied byte for byte. tools/release/press_badges.py pins their SHA-256 and fails
when either file changes.

The kit asks that the badge is used as it is (never recoloured, stretched or redrawn), with room around it, that the
name is written as one word, RecompLauncher, and that the badge links to the port's page:
https://recomplauncher.com/game/project-vekta
